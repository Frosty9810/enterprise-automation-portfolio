Local deterministic demonstration. Scores measure lexical token overlap, not confidence. This build has no trained reranker, calibrated probability, vector search or model benchmark. A retrieval advances this tenant’s ingestion watermark to at least its as-of instant. Later new events must have recordedAt at or after that watermark. Replaying an unchanged saved command remains allowed.
