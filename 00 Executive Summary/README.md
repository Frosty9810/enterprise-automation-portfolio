## Situation

This portfolio demonstrates how business rules, integration boundaries and operator recovery fit together. Its [complete inventory](../PROJECT-INVENTORY.md) distinguishes 27 automation examples, 20 life/data prototypes, nine engineering examples, twelve business-system views and five research tracks. The unified `/projects` page lists all 73 entries with their scope and status. Entries can share components; catalog counts do not establish independent production products.

## Complication

A successful script does not establish hosted integration acceptance or business impact. Contact planning, durable recovery and domain decisions have different evidence boundaries. Several industry flagships remain partial. [AtlasGraph](../demo-lab/ATLASGRAPH.md) provides bounded local structured-evidence compilation and graph traversal. [TrustGate](../demo-lab/TRUSTGATE.md) adds source-bound arguments, review and execution for three synthetic SQLite tools. [ChronosRank](../demo-lab/CHRONOSRANK.md) adds event-sourced temporal memory and deterministic retrieval with saved evidence. [DocksideSLM](../demo-lab/DOCKSIDE.md) adds supplied-field validation, reviewed offline delivery and a synthetic ERP receiver. [VeilRAG](../demo-lab/VEILRAG.md) adds a declared-literal alias boundary with an encrypted local mapping and a checked child fixture. All five full research blueprints remain unfinished. The inventory identifies those gaps instead of treating their names as completed implementations.

## Recommendation

Review three complementary cases first. Run them with synthetic data, introduce one failure, inspect the resulting hold or recovery and retain the exact source-bound evidence. Use the [review handoff](../REVIEW-HANDOFF.md) for branch dependencies and reproduction commands.
