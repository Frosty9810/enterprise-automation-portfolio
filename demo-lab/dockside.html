DOCKSIDESLM / A BOUNDED LOCAL BUILD

Receive carefully.
Deliver once.

Inspect a supplied document and its structured fields, record a local review, then follow a durable queue through an offline or interrupted delivery to a SQLite receiver.

03 / DELIVER

Exercise the durable local queue

An approved record can be attempted against the local receiver. An interrupted response leaves delivery uncertain; reconcile with the receiver before treating it as delivered. A retry must not create a second receiver record.
