## ROI Formula

```
Net Monthly Benefit = Total Monthly Benefit − Total Monthly Recurring Cost
Payback Period (months) = One-Time Cost ÷ Net Monthly Benefit
Annualized ROI (%) = (Net Monthly Benefit × 12 − One-Time Cost) ÷ One-Time Cost × 100
```
